HOW TO PLAY

/// THE BASICS

Type the falling letters before they hit the bottom. Missing a letter or word costs 1 LIFE. Lose all lives, and you're WASTED.

/// COMBOS & MULTIPLIERS

String consecutive hits together to build your COMBO. Every 6 consecutive hits increases your SCORE MULTIPLIER (x1 -> x2 -> x3...). Missing resets your combo!

/// COINS & WORDS

Earn COINS by playing. Normal letters give 1 coin. Sometimes, full WORDS will drop. Type them perfectly for +10 coins and massive bonus points! Spend coins in the tracklist to unlock new tracks.

/// POWER-UPS & HAZARDS

+ A + (1-UP): Grants an extra life (Max 5).

~ A ~ (SLOW): Triggers Time Warp, slightly slowing down everything for 2 seconds.

[ A ] (NUKE): Destroys all currently falling letters for massive points.

☠ A ☠ (POISON): DO NOT TYPE! Typing poison costs 1 life, halts your combo, and halves your session coins! Unhit items cause no penalty.
